Detects cell nuclei using blurring, rolling ball background subtraction, Triangle thresholding and hole filling, like the EVAnalyzer Fiji plugin (touching nuclei are not split). The class the objects get is the module's "Nuclei" parameter.

**Contributed by:** Melanie Schuerz (University of Salzburg)
