### Bug or feature request?

[github.com/evanalyzer/evanalyzer/issues](https://github.com/evanalyzer/evanalyzer/issues)

Search existing issues first — your bug or feature idea may already be tracked. If not, open a new issue with as much detail as possible (EVAnalyzer version, operating system, and steps to reproduce).
